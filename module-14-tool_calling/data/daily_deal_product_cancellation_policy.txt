DAILY DEAL
PRODUCT CANCELLATION POLICY

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

1. PURPOSE
Daily Deal permits customers to cancel eligible product orders before the order reaches a non-cancellable stage. This policy explains cancellation eligibility, request requirements, refunds, partial cancellations, and exceptions.

2. CANCELLATION ELIGIBILITY
A customer may request cancellation while the order is in one of these statuses:
- Pending
- Confirmed
- Processing

Cancellation is normally unavailable once the order has been packed for shipment, dispatched, handed to a delivery carrier, or delivered.

3. HOW TO CANCEL AN ORDER
Customers must contact Daily Deal customer support and provide:
- Order ID
- Customer name
- Registered email address or phone number
- Reason for cancellation, when requested

A cancellation request is not considered successful until Daily Deal confirms the cancellation.

4. CANCELLATION AFTER DISPATCH
If an order has already been dispatched, Daily Deal may not be able to stop the shipment. The customer may instead need to receive the product and use the applicable return or refund process, if the product qualifies under the relevant policy.

5. REFUNDS FOR CANCELLED ORDERS
When Daily Deal approves a cancellation, the amount paid for the cancelled product will be refunded according to the applicable payment method and refund processing timeline.

The exact time for the refund to appear in a bank account, card, or digital wallet depends on the payment provider. Processing delays caused by a payment provider do not mean that the cancellation was unsuccessful.

6. PARTIAL CANCELLATION
For orders containing multiple products, customers may request cancellation of individual products if those products are still eligible for cancellation. Cancelling one product does not automatically cancel the remaining products in the order.

For a partial cancellation, the refund is based on the cancelled product and any applicable charges or discounts.

7. COUPONS, PROMOTIONS, AND BUNDLE DISCOUNTS
If the cancelled product was purchased using a coupon, promotional offer, bundle discount, or other special pricing, Daily Deal may recalculate the discount.

If removing a product causes the order to lose eligibility for a promotion, the final refund may be reduced by the amount of discount that is no longer applicable.

8. CANCELLATION BY DAILY DEAL
Daily Deal may cancel an order when fulfillment is not possible or when an order contains a product availability issue, listing or pricing error, payment problem, suspected fraudulent activity, or other operational issue.

For a prepaid order cancelled by Daily Deal, the applicable amount paid will be refunded through the normal refund process.

9. IMPORTANT RULES
- A cancellation request does not guarantee approval.
- Cancellation is effective only after Daily Deal confirms it.
- The order status at the time of the request determines cancellation eligibility.
- Customers should retain the cancellation confirmation and Order ID.
- This policy covers product cancellation only and does not replace separate return, replacement, warranty, or refund policies.

10. CONTACT
For cancellation assistance, customers should contact Daily Deal customer support using the support channel shown with the order or provided on the Daily Deal website.

Daily Deal may update this policy when necessary. The policy version applicable at the time of the cancellation request will generally govern the request, subject to applicable law.
