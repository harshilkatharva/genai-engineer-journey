20 RAG Quality Questions
========================

1. A general-office employee is scheduled at 09:00 and arrives at 09:11. Which attendance rule applies, and why should the answer not be copied to a warehouse employee on a 07:00 shift?
2. An employee discovers an incorrect attendance entry eight working days later, but the attendance system was unavailable for two of those days. What evidence determines whether the late correction can still be evaluated?
3. A candidate requests a joining-date change 24 hours before joining. Which approval path is required, and what must the final record contain?
4. Does completing six months of probation automatically create a salary increase? Which policy distinction proves the answer?
5. A promotion is effective on the 21st while payroll closes on the 20th. Which date controls the salary change and which date controls processing?
6. What is the exact product code, memory, storage, price and warranty of the HP ProBook 440 G11 record?
7. Compare VLT-LAP-DE-5450-16 and VLT-LAP-DE-7440-16. Which one has the longer warranty class, and why is brand-only retrieval unsafe?
8. Which phone records have 512 GB storage, and what memory and demo price distinguish them?
9. Which 55-inch televisions are under ₹75,000 in the synthetic catalog, and which exact fields must be retrieved before recommending one?
10. Which AC is the only 2.0-ton entry, and does its catalog record require standard installation or an enhanced survey?
11. A quotation has seven calendar days of validity. Why is that rule different from the seven-day customer return-request window?
12. An enterprise purchase order is missing tax information and the shipping entity. Should the order team infer the fields or pause for correction?
13. A television is delivered Friday and installed Monday. What is the correct service state on Saturday?
14. A customer reports a cracked television panel 30 hours after delivery. What evidence and policy branch are relevant, and does the timing itself guarantee a refund?
15. A refund is marked initiated but the customer cannot see the credit in their bank. What exact distinction should the assistant explain?
16. A customer activates VLT-PHO-MOTO-E60E and asks for a change-of-mind return four days later. Which specific policy should outrank the generic seven-day statement?
17. A company-owned Dell laptop is lost by an employee. Why should Security/asset procedures be retrieved instead of customer return policy?
18. A supplier offers VLT-LAP-HP-640G11-32 as a substitute for VLT-LAP-HP-440G11-16. Can Procurement treat them as equivalent simply because both are HP business laptops?
19. A senior executive asks for production database access and says their title is enough approval. What does the security workflow actually require?
20. Why is the word 'approval' by itself a poor retrieval query in this corpus? Give at least four different approval contexts and the policy domain for each.
