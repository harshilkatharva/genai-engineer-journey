Wall-mount service
------------------
Wall-mount eligibility does not mean wall-mount service is included in every product price. The service can depend on the selected installation package, the site assessment, the mounting surface and the bracket category. A customer should receive a confirmed appointment and service line item before assuming that installation is bundled.

TV retrieval traps
------------------
'55-inch 4K TV' matches the Sony, TCL and Panasonic records. '65-inch QLED' points strongly to Samsung but panel and mounting details must still be read from the exact catalog entry. A generic request should not result in an invented port count or refresh rate because those details are not present in every record.
