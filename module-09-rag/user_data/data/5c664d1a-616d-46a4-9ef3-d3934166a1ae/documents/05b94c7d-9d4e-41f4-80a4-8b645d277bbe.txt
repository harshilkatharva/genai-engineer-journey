Phone accessories
-----------------
Virelia offers synthetic accessory codes such as VLT-PHA-USBC-65 and VLT-PHA-CAR-45. These accessories are not automatically bundled with the phone price. An accessory return can follow a different path from an activated device return.

Phone catalog retrieval traps
-----------------------------
'512 GB business phone' is not enough to uniquely identify a device because the Motorola and Xiaomi records both contain 512 GB storage, while their memory, price, brand and code differ. '12 GB Samsung' points toward one model in this file, whereas '12 GB Android phone' matches several models. Exact product answers should include evidence from the specific product record.
