Service lookup note
-------------------
Service codes represent chargeable or operational service items and should not be confused with product codes. A product can be purchased without a service code, while an installation case can contain both a product line and a service line.

Compatibility rule
------------------
Compatibility is directional evidence, not a universal bundle rule. The presence of VLT-DOCK-D450 in the accessory catalog does not imply that every laptop includes it, and the presence of VLT-TV-BRK-M2 does not imply that every TV includes wall installation.
