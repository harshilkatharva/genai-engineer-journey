Virelia Systems & Commerce — Company Overview
=============================================
Synthetic source document for RAG testing. Virelia Systems & Commerce Pvt. Ltd. is a fictional company used only to build a realistic retrieval corpus. Numbers, policies, product codes, offices, prices, names and timelines are invented for this dataset.

Business model
--------------
Virelia sells workplace technology and consumer electronics through direct enterprise sales, a web storefront, assisted retail orders, and regional fulfillment centers. The company also coordinates installation for selected televisions and air conditioners and operates a centralized repair and customer-support desk. The business therefore has several populations that use the same vocabulary differently: employees, customers, vendors, installers, warehouse staff, finance analysts and security personnel.

The company has intentionally separated policy ownership. People Operations controls employee rules. Finance controls payroll and financial records. Product Operations maintains catalog data. Customer Experience controls customer-facing commercial rules. Procurement manages suppliers. Information Security controls access and information-handling standards. A response that cites the wrong owner may sound plausible but should be treated as incorrect.

Corporate locations
-------------------
The registered headquarters is the Ahmedabad Central Office. The western logistics node is in Pune. Bengaluru hosts engineering support and selected enterprise implementation specialists. Hyderabad operates a client-integration desk. Jaipur is a small-region fulfillment hub for north-west orders. Indore is an overflow warehouse used during seasonal demand.

The presence of an office does not automatically determine an employee's attendance arrangement. An employee can be attached to a city while being classified as remote-eligible. Likewise, a customer order can be dispatched from one warehouse and installed by a technician from another region.

Internal identifiers
--------------------
Corporate documents use prefixes to make retrieval easier. HR-JOI references joining and onboarding. HR-ATT references attendance and leave. HR-SAL covers payroll and compensation. CAT identifies the product catalog. CX-PUR governs purchase and order handling. CX-RET covers returns and refunds. SEC covers security and information handling. PRC covers procurement and supplier operations.

Document references and product codes are distinct identifier classes. For example, HR-ATT-14 is a policy clause identifier, while VLT-LN-E14G6-16 is a catalog product code. The latter identifies a sellable configuration, not a policy.

Terminology map
---------------
A Customer is the commercial party on an order. A User is the person who actually operates the device and may be someone else. An Account Owner is the Virelia employee responsible for an enterprise relationship. An Order Number identifies the transaction. A Product Code identifies the catalog configuration. A Serial Number identifies an individual physical unit. A Service Ticket identifies an after-sales case.

'Approval' is not a single concept in Virelia. Manager approval can authorize a team-level operational action, Finance approval can authorize spend or payroll treatment, Security approval can authorize access, and customer acceptance can authorize an order. Retrieval should therefore privilege context over the token 'approval' itself.

Policy precedence
-----------------
When two instructions appear to conflict, the more specific rule normally controls the specific case. A product-specific return matrix can narrow a generic customer return rule. A regional attendance calendar can replace the generic holiday list for employees mapped to that region. A signed customer contract may contain a negotiated commercial term that differs from the default web-store procedure.

A casual statement from a salesperson, manager, or colleague is not an amendment to written policy. An exception should be recorded in the system associated with the transaction. The record should name the approver, the affected case, the date, and the duration of the exception when the exception is temporary.

Example: enterprise laptop order
--------------------------------
Customer Ardent Biosciences places an order for 14 Dell Latitude 5450 units and requests a separate on-site imaging service. The product catalog determines the exact laptop configuration. The customer-purchase policy determines how the purchase order is validated. Procurement policy is relevant to the supplier side, not to the customer order merely because the same laptops originated from a vendor. The service request is recorded separately so that device configuration work is not confused with delivery confirmation.

One unit arrives with a damaged carton. That unit follows the damaged-shipment workflow. The remaining thirteen units are not automatically placed on hold unless the inspection identifies a broader lot problem.

Example: employee reimbursement
-------------------------------
A field engineer buys an approved HDMI adapter personally and later requests reimbursement. The reimbursement policy governs the employee's expense claim. The customer return policy is irrelevant unless the employee is separately acting as a customer in a commercial purchase. If the adapter was purchased from Virelia's store, an exchange or return might exist as a customer matter, but that does not prove reimbursement eligibility.

Example: remote employee in an office city
------------------------------------------
Mira is assigned to the Bengaluru office but has a remote-eligible employment classification. On a Wednesday she works from home in Bengaluru. Her attendance remains governed by the remote-work procedure attached to her employee record. The existence of the Bengaluru office does not convert a remote workday into an unauthorized absence.

The same employee later visits the office for an approved team session. That physical visit does not by itself change the employee's formal work arrangement.

Example: refund and bank credit
-------------------------------
A customer receives an email stating that a refund was initiated on 14 September. The event indicates that Virelia has started the refund through the approved payment route. It does not prove that the customer's bank or card issuer has already posted the amount. Support agents should distinguish refund initiation from financial-settlement visibility.

Data quality expectations
-------------------------
Catalog teams must preserve product-code integrity. A brand name, model family, and physical serial should not be used interchangeably. Customer support should capture the exact order number and product code before promising a policy outcome. HR should use the employee's assigned record rather than an informal spreadsheet when determining attendance or leave state.

What this corpus deliberately tests
-----------------------------------
The corpus contains long documents, section headings, exact codes, policy exceptions, near-duplicate concepts with different meanings, and references that cross documents without collapsing them into one rule. Some answers require a single clause. Others require two pieces of evidence, such as a product code from the catalog plus a return condition from the customer policy. Good retrieval should rank the precise source above semantically related distractors.
