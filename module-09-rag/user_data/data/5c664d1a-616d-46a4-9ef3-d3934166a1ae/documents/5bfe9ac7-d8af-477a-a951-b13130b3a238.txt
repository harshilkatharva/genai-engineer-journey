Client Policy — Terms, Warranty & Support
=========================================
Synthetic terms, warranty and customer-support rules.

CX-TC-01 — Customer relationship
--------------------------------
Customers can purchase as individuals or as legal entities. The order record defines which commercial party owns the transaction. The end user can be different from the invoiced customer.

CX-TC-02 — Warranty scope
-------------------------
Warranty coverage depends on product, serial number, date, region and the applicable warranty class. Product-code data alone may identify the general model, but the physical unit's serial is often necessary for service validation.

CX-TC-03 — Warranty versus return
---------------------------------
A return is a commercial disposition process. A warranty case addresses product performance or defects under the applicable coverage. The two should not be treated as synonyms.

CX-TC-04 — Service ticket creation
----------------------------------
Customers opening support cases should provide the order number, product code and serial number where available. The team may ask for safe verification details, but customers should never publish passwords or payment authentication codes in the ticket.

CX-TC-05 — Support identity verification
----------------------------------------
Before discussing protected account details, support may verify authorized contact information. Knowledge of an order number alone may not be enough when the request involves sensitive account changes.

CX-TC-06 — Installation support
-------------------------------
Installation tickets can track appointment date, technician assignment, site readiness and completion. Delivery completion and installation completion are different events.

CX-TC-07 — Service escalation
-----------------------------
A support case can be escalated for technical review when frontline troubleshooting cannot resolve the issue. Escalation does not automatically grant a refund or replacement entitlement.

CX-TC-08 — Replacement
----------------------
Replacement decisions depend on product condition, service assessment, inventory and applicable policy. A customer request for replacement is a request to be evaluated, not automatic authorization.

CX-TC-09 — Service appointment no-show
--------------------------------------
If a customer misses an installation appointment, the team may need to rebook it. Rebooking availability depends on technician capacity and region.

CX-TC-10 — Communication record
-------------------------------
Material customer commitments should be recorded in the order or service system. An unlogged promise from an individual agent should not be assumed to modify written terms.

CX-TC-11 — Product description
------------------------------
Catalog descriptions help identify a product configuration but do not supersede the detailed order record where the exact product code, accessory set or service line is specified.

CX-TC-12 — Promotional conditions
---------------------------------
Promotional bundles can include an accessory, installation discount or service credit. The conditions should be retrieved from the promotion attached to the order, not inferred from a product page that was valid for another campaign.

CX-TC-13 — Privacy in support
-----------------------------
Customer information collected for support should be limited to what is necessary for the case. Agents should not request unrelated personal details simply because those details are visible in another system.

CX-TC-14 — Data deletion request
--------------------------------
A customer request to delete information may be subject to legal, financial or contractual retention obligations. Support should route the request rather than promise immediate deletion.

CX-TC-15 — Service fees
-----------------------
Some installation or diagnostic services carry fees. A fee should be confirmed from the applicable service catalog or customer agreement.

CX-TC-16 — Support hours
------------------------
General support and specialized technical support can have different operating hours. A customer asking for an urgent technical callback should be given the hours associated with the relevant queue rather than a generic office schedule.

CX-TC-17 — Case closure
-----------------------
A case may close after customer confirmation, successful service completion, or a documented resolution path. Closure of a support ticket does not erase the underlying order or warranty record.

CX-TC-18 — Evidence
-------------------
Photos, diagnostic logs and technician findings can support a case. Evidence should be linked to the ticket so that another agent can review the history without relying on a private conversation.

CX-TC-19 — Escalation to Finance
--------------------------------
Refund-status questions may require Finance or payments involvement. Customer support can relay the case while keeping the distinction between operational case handling and actual payment processing.

CX-TC-20 — Terms hierarchy
--------------------------
Where a customer contract contains a specific service commitment, that recorded agreement may take precedence over a generic web-store statement for that customer and order.
