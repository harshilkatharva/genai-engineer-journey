HR — Joining & Onboarding Policy
================================
Synthetic HR policy for testing long-context retrieval. All names and dates are fictional.

HR-JOI-01 — Offer acceptance
----------------------------
Once a candidate accepts an offer, the recruiter moves the candidate into the pre-joining workflow. Offer acceptance does not grant production-system access and does not constitute completion of onboarding. The candidate receives a joining packet containing the confirmed joining date, reporting instructions, required documents, contact details for People Operations, and the security acknowledgement.

If the candidate identifies an error in the offer record, People Operations should correct the source record rather than creating a parallel note. For example, a typo in the candidate's legal name should be corrected before payroll setup.

HR-JOI-02 — Joining-date change
-------------------------------
A joining date can be changed when business or personal circumstances justify it. Requests made more than three business days before the planned start date are reviewed by the recruiter and hiring manager. Requests inside that window are escalated to the People Operations regional lead because access provisioning and manager availability may already have been scheduled.

The approval record must preserve the original date, proposed date, request reason category, requestor, approver and timestamp. A recruiter may recommend a change but may not silently modify the date after the final offer confirmation.

HR-JOI-03 — Identity verification
---------------------------------
Before unrestricted system provisioning, the new employee must complete identity verification through the approved process. The company does not ask applicants to disclose account passwords, banking PINs or one-time authentication codes. Bank-account ownership can be verified through the designated payroll workflow without requesting a personal banking password.

If verification is pending, People Operations may permit limited onboarding activities that do not expose restricted company systems. The access decision should be recorded in the onboarding ticket.

HR-JOI-04 — First working day
-----------------------------
The first working day includes identity confirmation, policy acknowledgement, workplace or remote-work orientation, manager introduction, role briefing and the creation of baseline HR records. Equipment allocation, where applicable, is tied to the employee's role and site.

A remote employee receives the same policy material as a site-based employee. The delivery mechanism differs, but the employee must still acknowledge the required information-security controls.

HR-JOI-05 — Equipment handover
------------------------------
A company laptop is issued against an asset record. The record contains an internal asset identifier, serial number, employee owner, issue date, condition at issue and return expectation. A laptop can be physically present in the office without being assigned to the employee; the asset record is the authoritative assignment reference.

HR-JOI-06 — Probation
---------------------
The normal probation period is six calendar months unless the employment agreement states a different period. The probation review is a performance and fit assessment. It is not the same event as the annual compensation cycle.

A manager may recommend confirmation before the end of the normal period when the employment terms permit it, but early confirmation requires a documented review. Completion of probation does not create an automatic fixed salary percentage increase.

HR-JOI-07 — Joining exceptions
------------------------------
If an employee cannot access the onboarding portal on the first day because of a verified technical outage, People Operations may record the pending steps through the fallback ticket. The fallback process should capture who confirmed the information and which original workflow item remains open.

If the person simply forgets to complete a required form, the issue is treated as an incomplete onboarding task rather than a technical exception.

HR-JOI-08 — Interns and apprentices
-----------------------------------
Interns and apprentices may follow a shortened onboarding sequence, but they still need identity verification, confidentiality acknowledgement, security orientation, and a designated supervisor. Their access should be restricted to systems necessary for the assignment. Completion of a learning plan is not equivalent to employment confirmation.

HR-JOI-09 — Transfers before joining
------------------------------------
A candidate may be moved to another team before joining. The recruiter must update the source offer and provisioning requests together. It is not sufficient to change the manager in a chat message while leaving the original role in the HR record because role-based system access can depend on the recorded job profile.

HR-JOI-10 — No-show handling
----------------------------
If a candidate does not appear on the confirmed joining date and gives no notice, People Operations marks the case for no-show review. The recruiter should document attempted contact. A no-show status is not the same as a rejected offer; the underlying candidate status depends on the eventual decision and written communication.

HR-JOI-11 — Evidence rules
--------------------------
Onboarding evidence should be stored in the approved HR repository. Screenshots can supplement an official record but should not replace a required source record when the workflow supports structured capture. The person conducting the verification is responsible for ensuring that the record is attributable and timestamped.

HR-JOI-12 — Regional calendars
------------------------------
A new employee's joining date can coincide with a regional holiday. People Operations must use the calendar associated with the employee's assigned work location when scheduling a start-day experience. A holiday in Gujarat does not automatically make  the same date a holiday in Karnataka or Telangana.

HR-JOI-13 — Manager responsibilities
------------------------------------
The hiring manager prepares a first-week plan, confirms the role objective, identifies mandatory learning and ensures that a suitable workstation or remote setup is available. The manager should not bypass People Operations or Security controls by manually granting access.

HR-JOI-14 — Joining checklist dependencies
------------------------------------------
Payroll activation, badge provisioning, application access and equipment handover can have different prerequisites. A candidate may therefore be fully joined while a non-critical application remains pending. Support should report the exact pending dependency rather than labeling the whole onboarding process as incomplete.

HR-JOI-15 — Data correction after joining
-----------------------------------------
If an employee discovers a typo in a personal record after joining, the correction should be made in the system of record and propagated through the approved integration. Employees should not maintain competing personal-information copies and ask each department to treat a different version as authoritative.

HR-JOI-16 — Temporary work location
-----------------------------------
A new hire who wants to work temporarily from another city must obtain the appropriate approval before relying on that arrangement. Travel convenience is not itself a remote-work authorization. The relevant question is the employee's assigned work mode and the applicable temporary-location rule.

HR-JOI-17 — Security acknowledgement
------------------------------------
Security acknowledgement is a mandatory onboarding step for roles with company-system access. The acknowledgement records that the employee has received the required security guidance; it does not grant permissions by itself. Permissions remain subject to the access-request workflow.

HR-JOI-18 — Joining date vs payroll date
----------------------------------------
The joining date is the employee's confirmed employment start date. The payroll processing date is an accounting event. They should never be treated as synonyms. A person can join before the payroll close and appear in the same month's payroll or join after the close and appear in the following cycle depending on cut-offs.

HR-JOI-19 — Special approval
----------------------------
Executive hires and employees joining roles with privileged access may have additional approvals. These additional approvals supplement, rather than replace, standard identity verification and policy acknowledgements.

HR-JOI-20 — Policy references
-----------------------------
HR-JOI-20 must be interpreted alongside HR-ATT and SEC when a joining case concerns attendance or system access. The existence of a reference does not mean that all provisions from the referenced policy are imported into the joining rule.


HR-JOI-21 — Example: delayed verification
-----------------------------------------
A candidate has accepted the offer but the identity-verification appointment is delayed because the verification provider is unavailable. People Operations can keep the case open and schedule a new slot. The delay does not justify bypassing identity controls.


HR-JOI-22 — Example: remote equipment
-------------------------------------
A remote joiner receives a laptop by courier before the first working day. Receipt of the laptop is an equipment event, not proof that the employee has completed all joining formalities. The asset record and onboarding record remain separate.


HR-JOI-23 — Example: role change
--------------------------------
The hiring manager changes an analyst's role to a senior analyst before day one. The role change should be reflected in the source employment record because system access may depend on the revised profile.


HR-JOI-24 — Example: missing bank record
----------------------------------------
An employee joins successfully but the payroll bank workflow remains incomplete. People Operations should identify the pending payroll dependency rather than mark the complete onboarding process as failed.


HR-JOI-25 — Example: regional office closure
--------------------------------------------
If the assigned office is unexpectedly closed on the first day, the employee can follow the emergency remote-joining instruction. This does not permanently change the employee's work-mode classification.


HR-JOI-26 — Example: contractor conversion
------------------------------------------
A contractor converting to regular employment must complete the employee onboarding requirements applicable to the new role. Prior contractor access is not evidence that employee access is automatically approved.


HR-JOI-27 — Example: document correction
----------------------------------------
A passport number is mistyped in an onboarding form. The correction should update the source record and preserve an audit trail. Creating a second employee record is not an acceptable workaround.


HR-JOI-28 — Example: first-week absence
---------------------------------------
A new employee who needs leave during the first week should use the normal leave and manager-approval process unless an emergency procedure applies. Being new does not create a separate undocumented leave category.
